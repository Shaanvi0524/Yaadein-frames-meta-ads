# Yaadein Frames – Meta Ads Lead Generation Campaign

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a practical Meta Ads Lead Generation campaign created for Yaadein Frames.

The campaign was designed to promote Yaadein Frames products, reach relevant customers through Facebook and Instagram, and generate potential customer enquiries.

## Campaign Objective

The main objectives of the campaign were:

- Generate potential customer leads
- Promote Yaadein Frames products
- Increase brand awareness
- Reach relevant customers through Facebook and Instagram
- Measure and analyse real advertising campaign performance

## Campaign Details

| Metric | Details |
|---|---|
| Campaign Name | Yaadein Frames – Lead Generation |
| Campaign Objective | Lead Generation |
| Ad Set | Yaadein Frames – Frame Business Ad |
| Daily Budget | ₹100 |
| Campaign Duration | 1 October 2026 – 5 October 2026 |
| Amount Spent | ₹394.45 |
| Results | 23 |
| Cost per Result | ₹17.15 |
| Impressions | 4,359 |
| Reach | 3,073 |

## Campaign Performance

The Yaadein Frames Meta Ads campaign generated *23 results* during the campaign period.

A total amount of *₹394.45* was spent with a daily budget of *₹100. The campaign achieved **4,359 impressions* and reached *3,073 people*.

The average cost per result was *₹17.15*, providing practical insight into the cost-effectiveness of the lead generation campaign.

## Performance Summary
